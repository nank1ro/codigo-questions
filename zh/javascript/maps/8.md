# --solutions--

```javascript
const capitals = new Map([["Italy", "Rome"], ["France", "Paris"]]);
for (const [country, city] of capitals) {
  console.log(`${city} is the capital of ${country}`);
}
console.log(capitals.size);
```

# --output--

Rome is the capital of Italy
Paris is the capital of France
2
